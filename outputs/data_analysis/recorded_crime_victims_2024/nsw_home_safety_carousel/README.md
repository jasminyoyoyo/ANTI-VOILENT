# NSW home-safety Instagram carousel

Six-slide Chinese-language Instagram carousel based on police-recorded NSW
FDV-related assault victim data for 2024.

## Source cells

- Female FDV-related assault victim count and FDV share of all female assault
  victims: ABS Recorded Crime – Victims 2024, Table 30, Excel row 19.
- Relationship and residential-location proportions: Table 33, NSW female
  column.

## Key values

- 23,518 female FDV-related assault victim records
- 65.0% of all female assault victims recorded by NSW Police were FDV-related
- 87.2% of female FDV-related assault victim records had a residential location
- 59.4% intimate partner
- 35.2% other family member
- 5.0% other specified FDV relationship

The carousel deliberately frames “Is home safer?” as a question, not a proven
causal conclusion. Residential location does not necessarily mean the victim's
own home.
