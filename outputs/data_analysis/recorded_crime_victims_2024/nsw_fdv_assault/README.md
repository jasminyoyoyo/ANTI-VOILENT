# NSW police-recorded FDV-related assault victims, 2014–2024

## Measure

Victims of family and domestic violence (FDV) related assault recorded by New
South Wales Police, as published by the Australian Bureau of Statistics.

This is not a count of calls to police, all FDV incidents, or unique people
across the whole time series. It is an annual administrative victim count for
the selected offence.

## Source

- Australian Bureau of Statistics, *Recorded Crime – Victims, 2024*
- Workbook: `ABS Recorded Crime Victims 2024 FDV Tables 29 to 38.xlsx`
- Sheet: `Table 30`
- NSW, Persons, Assault: Excel row 26
- Number: columns B–L
- Victimisation rate: columns M–W
- FDV share of all assault victims: columns X–AH
- Release date: 3 September 2025

Official page:

https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/crime-and-justice/recorded-crime-victims/latest-release

## Comparability and quality notes

- ABS states that NSW FDV data from 2021 may not be comparable with earlier
  years.
- Cells are randomly adjusted by ABS to protect confidentiality; discrepancies
  may occur between components and totals.
- FDV status is based on a specified family/domestic relationship in police
  records or an FDV flag following police investigation.
- The rate is per 100,000 NSW residents.
