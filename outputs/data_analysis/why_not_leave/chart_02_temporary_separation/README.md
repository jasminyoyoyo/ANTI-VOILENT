# Chart 02: Leaving was not always a single event

## Takeaway

Among an estimated 1.3647 million women who experienced violence by a former partner while living together, approximately 583,800 temporarily separated from that partner. This represents 42.8% of the cohort.

Temporary separation shows that many women created distance from a violent relationship. The data do not establish that every temporary separation was intended to be permanent, and should not be interpreted as proof that leaving was safe, simple or fully within one person's control.

## Source detail

- Temporarily separated: 583,800 women; 42.8%; estimate RSE 5.2%; proportion RSE 4.2%.
- Once only: 218,900 women; 16.0%; estimate RSE 9.5%; proportion RSE 9.0%.
- More than once: 357,900 women; 26.2%; estimate RSE 6.4%; proportion RSE 5.6%.
- Did not temporarily separate: 781,700 women; 57.3%.
- Denominator: 1,364,700 women who experienced violence by a former partner while living together.

The once-only and more-than-once components sum to 42.2%, while the published total is 42.8%. ABS randomly adjusts cells to protect confidentiality, so components may not sum exactly to totals. Do not silently force the components to add to 42.8%.
