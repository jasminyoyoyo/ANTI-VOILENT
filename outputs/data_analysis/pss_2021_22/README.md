## Interpretation notes

- Population: women aged 18 years and over.
- Experience window: since age 15.
- Jurisdiction means current residence at the time of the survey, not necessarily where the experience occurred.
- Values are survey-based estimated prevalence percentages, not police-recorded crime rates.
- All estimates displayed in these figures have a relative standard error below 25%.
- Small differences between jurisdictions may not be statistically meaningful.
