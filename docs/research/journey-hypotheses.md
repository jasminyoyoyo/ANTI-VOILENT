# User Journey Hypotheses

These are hypotheses to validate through research.

## Journey 1: I Might Be Unsafe Tonight

### Motivation

The user is worried the situation could escalate soon.

### Emotional State

Fearful, rushed, uncertain, possibly hiding phone use.

### First 10 Seconds

Needs a direct path:

- what to do now
- when to call 000
- who to contact
- where to move
- what to prepare

### Product Output

Tonight Safety Checklist

### Possible CTA

`I might be unsafe tonight`

## Journey 2: I Need To Prepare To Leave

### Motivation

The user is not leaving immediately but wants to prepare without being discovered.

### Emotional State

Afraid, cautious, planning, overwhelmed.

### First 10 Seconds

Needs reassurance that they do not need to decide everything now.

### Product Output

Leave Preparation Checklist

Includes:

- documents
- money
- transport
- children
- medication
- trusted contact
- safe device

### Possible CTA

`I need to prepare to leave`

## Journey 3: My Phone May Be Monitored

### Motivation

The user suspects someone checks their phone, location, messages, or accounts.

### Emotional State

Anxious, suspicious, afraid of leaving traces.

### First 10 Seconds

Needs digital safety guidance without long explanations.

### Product Output

Phone Monitoring Check

Includes:

- location sharing
- account login sessions
- notification previews
- shared cloud accounts
- browser history
- safer device advice

### Possible CTA

`My phone may be monitored`

## Journey 4: I Am Not Sure If This Is Abuse

### Motivation

The user is confused by control, isolation, threats, or fear.

### Emotional State

Doubtful, embarrassed, defensive, conflicted.

### First 10 Seconds

Needs gentle language and no diagnosis.

### Product Output

Pattern Check + Next Step

### Possible CTA

`I am not sure if this is abuse`

## Journey 5: I Am Helping Someone Else

### Motivation

The user is worried about a friend, student, colleague, or family member.

### Emotional State

Concerned, unsure, afraid of making things worse.

### First 10 Seconds

Needs a safe way to help without forcing action.

### Product Output

Help Someone Else Guide

Includes:

- what to say
- what not to say
- when to call emergency services
- safe word planning
- sharing support options safely

### Possible CTA

`I am helping someone else`

## Journey 6: I Need Human Support

### Motivation

The user is ready to contact a real service.

### Emotional State

Urgent, tired, ready, scared.

### First 10 Seconds

Needs trusted contact options.

### Product Output

Find Human Help

Includes:

- 000
- 1800RESPECT
- legal support
- accommodation
- counselling
- local/community support

### Possible CTA

`Find human support`
