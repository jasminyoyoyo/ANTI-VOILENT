# Beacon Research Pack

This folder is for product research before committing to feature direction or interface design.

The next product decision should come from Australian context, user motivation, service gaps, and evidence:

> Why would someone open Beacon, and what do they need within the first 10 seconds?

## Research Files

- `research-first-product-discovery-bilingual.md` - research-first bilingual framework that derives product direction from Australian context, user segments, and unmet needs rather than current features.
- `research-pack-bilingual.md` - consolidated bilingual Chinese/English research pack.
- `research-plan.md` - research goals, methods, participants, ethics, and timeline.
- `research-questions.md` - the main questions we need to answer.
- `interview-guide.md` - interview scripts for support workers, bystanders, and lived-experience participants.
- `survey-questions.md` - lightweight survey questions for validating landing motivations.
- `competitor-analysis-template.md` - structure for reviewing comparable services and products.
- `journey-hypotheses.md` - likely user journeys and first-screen needs.
- `feature-validation-matrix.md` - how to decide which features to keep, remove, or redesign.

## Key Product Hypothesis

Beacon should not be positioned as a generic AI support website.

Stronger positioning:

> A discreet safety action tool for people in controlling or unsafe relationships.

The research should validate whether users need:

- Tonight Safety
- Leave Preparation
- Phone Monitoring Check
- Safe Word / Help Message
- Evidence Timeline
- Find Human Help
- Not Sure / Risk Check
- Help Someone Else

## Important Safety Note

Do not interview people currently in immediate danger unless the study is run with appropriate safeguarding, referral pathways, consent procedures, and experienced support partners. Start with desk research, support workers, and low-risk usability testing.
